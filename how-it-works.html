Common Questions

Answers before you Ask

Still have questions? Our team is happy to walk you through any part of the process in detail.

Learn More >
How quickly can my team be operational? >
Most teams are up and running within 2–4 weeks of your discovery call, depending on role complexity and headcount. We'll walk you through a clear timeline before we begin.
What happens if an associate isn't the right fit? >
We'll work with you to understand what's missing and replace the associate at no extra cost, so your operations stay on track without disruption.
Can I scale my team up or down? >
Yes. Your team can flex with demand — add associates during peak periods or scale back when things quiet down, with no long-term lock-in.
Do I manage the team directly? >
You set the direction and priorities; we handle day-to-day operations, coaching, and performance monitoring, keeping you looped in at every phase.
